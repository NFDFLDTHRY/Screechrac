# Facilitator — Project Manifest

**Version 1.1**

> The first real-world application built on top of the FSL (Foundational System Loop)
> cognitive engine. Facilitator is a **driver-first local problem-solving marketplace**.

---

## 1. Core Promise

> **"You have a problem. We fix it."**

Facilitator matches people who need something done — food delivery, roadside
assistance, labor help, moving, errands, contractor support, and more — with
independent drivers and workers who have **wheels and/or muscle**.

Everything starts as **free-form requests** and naturally evolves into clean,
repeatable **preset job types** based on real usage data.

---

## 2. Core Philosophy & Principles

1. **Driver-First in every decision.** Every feature, policy, and UI choice must
   improve driver life.
2. **Maximum Control, Minimum Bullshit.** No forced batches, no map hijacking, no
   hidden information.
3. **Sell Solutions, Not Tasks.** Customers describe problems; the platform and
   **The Listener** turn them into clear, priced jobs.
4. **Data-Driven Standardization.** Free-form requests evolve into one-tap preset
   buttons once variables are fully defined through real usage.
5. **Build a Real Support Network.** The Listener, Hive Calls, and Guardian Mode turn
   isolated gig workers into a connected, intelligent community.

---

## 3. Key Systems

### 3.1 The Listener
An always-on Grok voice agent that wakes when the driver goes online. It captures
customer notes, building access instructions, complaints, and weird delivery details
via voice.

### 3.2 Customer Mode
Driver says a trigger phrase → hands the phone to the customer for **direct voice
escalation to a human representative**, with the **FSL cognitive system actively
facilitating and recording the interaction**.

### 3.3 Job Creation
A single **"Post a Job"** button for customers — free-form **voice or text** that uses
the **FSL engine + The Listener** to turn a described problem into a clear, priced job.

### 3.4 Future Enhancements
- **Driver Shadow Intelligence Layer** — contextual hints, batching suggestions, and
  situational awareness for drivers.
- **Restaurant QR pipeline** — scan a restaurant QR to open a preset pickup job.

These are part of the vision and may be implemented as future enhancements.

---

## 4. Insurance Rules

- Contractors must carry **personal auto liability insurance**.
- **COIs (Certificates of Insurance)** are required only for contracts that explicitly
  mandate them.
- **No other insurance subsidies or platform-paid coverage** at this time.

---

## 5. Relationship to FSL

Facilitator is a **thin presentation and job-routing layer** on top of the FSL
cognitive engine. It introduces **no changes** to the FSL core:

- Every posted job is a **root node** (a *cable*, in the cable/strand/bulge spatial
  methodology) — the primary carrier axis a problem travels along.
- The clarifications, observations, and unknowns a job accrues are **strands** attached
  radially by degree-of-separation.
- The Listener / Customer-Mode escalation is a **flow** — the sole connective primitive
  by which structure crosses between customer and human representative.
- The FSL **Ledger remains the source of truth**; Facilitator only navigates and
  presents it.

The FSL Coherence Contract and the three canonical conceptual reference documents
remain the immutable source of truth and are never altered by this application.
